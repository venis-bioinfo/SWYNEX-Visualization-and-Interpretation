## Interpretation of charts

1. **Distribution plots:** Show the spread, concentration, and possible skewness of numerical values.
2. **Correlation heatmap:** Shows the direction and strength of linear associations between numerical variables. Correlation does not establish causation.
3. **Boxplots:** Show medians, quartiles, and potential outliers. A potential outlier is not automatically an error.
4. **Category charts:** Show the most frequent observed categories in the dataset.

## Limitations

- Visual patterns alone do not prove cause and effect.
- Results describe this dataset and may not generalize to other populations.
- Correlation may be affected by confounding variables or unusual observations.
- Statistical significance and causal claims require additional appropriate analyses.
